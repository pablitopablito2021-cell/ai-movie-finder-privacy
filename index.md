\# Privacy Policy for Ai Movie Finder



\*\*Effective date:\*\* June 2026  

\*\*App name:\*\* Ai Movie Finder  

\*\*Developer / Publisher:\*\* Ai Movie Finder Studio  

\*\*Contact email:\*\* moviefinderai.dev@gmail.com



Ai Movie Finder helps users identify movies and TV shows from short video clips or shared video links. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and what choices users have.



\## 1. Information we collect



\### Account information



When you sign in with Google, we may collect and store:



\- Firebase user ID

\- Google display name

\- Google email address

\- Google profile photo URL

\- Sign-in date/time

\- Account plan status, such as free or premium



We use this information to create your account, keep you signed in, manage scan limits, show your profile, and provide account-based features.



\### Uploaded or shared video content



When you use Ai Movie Finder to identify a movie or show, the app may process:



\- A video clip you upload

\- A video shared from another app

\- A short screen capture preview after you approve Android’s screen capture permission prompt

\- Shared text, caption text, or URL text related to the clip, when available



This content is used to identify the movie or show and return results such as title, poster, cast, description, rating, and watch-provider information.



\### Search, history, and watchlist data



If you use the app, we may store:



\- Search/analyze history

\- Movie result information

\- Watchlist items

\- Scan count and quota usage

\- Scan status, such as uploaded, complete, or failed



History and Watchlist features may be available only to Premium users.



\### Payment and subscription information



If you purchase Premium, payment processing is handled by Google Play and/or RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.



We may store subscription-related status such as:



\- Whether Premium is active

\- Subscription entitlement status

\- Purchase/restore status

\- App user ID linked to the purchase



\### Technical information



We may collect technical information needed to operate and debug the app, such as:



\- App activity related to scans

\- Error messages

\- Upload/analyze status

\- Device/app interactions needed for the screen capture feature

\- Backend request status



\## 2. How we use information



We use collected information to:



\- Sign users in and keep accounts persistent

\- Provide free and premium scan limits

\- Analyze clips and identify movies or TV shows

\- Show results, cast, descriptions, ratings, and watch-provider information

\- Save History and Watchlist items

\- Process Premium status and restore purchases

\- Prevent abuse of scan limits

\- Improve app reliability and fix errors

\- Provide user support



\## 3. Screen capture permission



Ai Movie Finder may ask for Android screen capture permission when you share a social video link instead of a direct video file.



Screen capture is used only after you approve the Android system permission prompt. The purpose is to capture a short preview of the shared clip so the app can identify the movie or show. The app does not use screen capture for unrelated background monitoring.



\## 4. How long we keep data



Uploaded clips may be temporarily uploaded for analysis and are intended to be deleted after processing when cleanup succeeds.



We may keep account data, scan records, History, Watchlist items, quota usage, and subscription status while your account is active or as needed to provide the app.



Some technical logs or failed cleanup records may remain for debugging, abuse prevention, or operational purposes.



\## 5. Sharing of information



We do not sell user personal information.



We may share or process information with service providers needed to run the app, including:



\- Google Firebase, for authentication, database, and storage

\- Google Play, for Android app distribution and payments

\- RevenueCat, for subscription management, if enabled

\- AI analysis providers, to analyze video frames and identify titles

\- TMDB or related movie-data services, to fetch movie/show metadata and watch-provider information



These services process data as needed to provide app functionality.
