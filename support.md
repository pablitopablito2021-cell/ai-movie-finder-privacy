# Movie Finder AI Support

Need help with Movie Finder AI on iPhone or Android? Write to us and we will get back to you.

**Email:** [moviefinderai.dev@gmail.com](mailto:moviefinderai.dev@gmail.com)

Please include your **Support ID**. You can find it in the app under **Profile**; tap it to copy it. It helps us look up your scans and your subscription.

## Common questions

### How do I identify a movie?

- Open a video in TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube, tap **Share** and choose **Movie Finder**, or
- In the app, tap **Analyze a video clip** and pick a short clip from your phone (up to 70 MB).

### The app did not recognize my clip

Recognition works best when the clip shows the actual scene clearly. Try a different part of the scene, or add an actor you recognized and scan again.

### A link could not be downloaded

Some videos cannot be fetched from a link. Save the video to your phone and choose it from Photos or Files instead.

### How many scans do I get?

The first 5 scans are free. Movie Finder Premium gives you 30 scans every day.

### How do I cancel or manage Premium?

- **iPhone:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Movie Finder Premium.
- **Android:** Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

### I subscribed but the app still shows the free plan

Open the subscribe screen and tap **Restore purchases**. If that does not help, email us with your Support ID.

### How do I delete my data?

History and favorites are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the app. To have server-side data for your install removed, email us with your Support ID.
